# Code of Conduct

Veloce is built and used by people from many backgrounds and skill levels. This
document sets the standard for how everyone taking part in the project -
reporting issues, sending pull requests, joining discussions, or helping others -
is expected to behave, and what to do when that standard is not met.

## The standard we hold

Work here on the assumption that the person on the other side is acting in good
faith and wants the project to succeed. In practice that means:

- Be direct about code and ideas, and considerate about people. Critique the
  patch, not the author.
- Assume competence and ask before assuming intent. A misunderstanding is far
  more common than bad faith.
- Welcome newcomers and questions. Everyone was new to the codebase once, and a
  patient answer is worth more than a clever dismissal.
- Accept that decisions are made on technical merit, and that a maintainer may
  decline a change without it being a judgement of the person who proposed it.
- Keep disagreements about the work. When you are wrong, say so; when someone
  else is, correct the work without belittling them.

## What is not acceptable

The following have no place in any project space, regardless of who does it or
how skilled they are:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Demeaning, discriminatory, or sexualised comments, or unwelcome attention of
  any kind.
- Insults aimed at a person rather than their argument, sustained disruption of
  discussion, or deliberately derailing the work of others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct a reasonable participant would read as hostile or exclusionary.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project occupies - the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any channel where you are representing Veloce or
acting as a participant in it.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this standard, report it
privately to the maintainer at **lokeshtallapaneni@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled discreetly; the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response proportionate to
what happened. Depending on severity that may be a private warning, a request
for a public or private apology, temporary exclusion from project spaces, or a
permanent ban from participation. The maintainer's decision on enforcement is
final.

Acting on a report in good faith will never count against you. Knowingly filing
a false or retaliatory report is itself a violation of this code.
